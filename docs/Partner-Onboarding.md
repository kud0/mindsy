# Welcome to Mindsy
## Partner Onboarding - Branding & Marketing

---

## What We Are

**Mindsy is a Lecture Copilot for students.**

We help students **learn and pass their exams** by turning lecture recordings into interactive study materials. Think of us as a co-pilot that sits alongside students throughout their academic journey - from attending lectures to exam day.

### The Simple Version
Students upload audio, video, or YouTube links of lectures → We transcribe and generate study materials → Students learn effectively and ace their exams.

---

## Core Purpose

**Help students learn and pass exams.**

Everything we build serves this single goal. We're not trying to be a note-taking app, or a productivity tool, or a social network. We're laser-focused on helping students succeed academically.

---

## What We Provide

### 1. **Hub (Main Dashboard)**
The student's home base with at-a-glance widgets:
- Profile
- Recent lectures
- Upcoming exams
- Pomodoro timer
- Schedule view
- Essay assignments
- Study stats (hours studied, lectures completed, average scores, streaks)

*Think: Command center for their academic life*

### 2. **Lectures**
Students upload lectures (audio, video, YouTube) and we organize them:
- Upload any lecture format
- Organize in folders (by course, semester, subject)
- See processing status
- Quick access to all lectures

### 3. **Student Desk** (The Core Experience)
When a student opens a lecture, they get:

**Three viewing modes:**
- **Transcript** - Full lecture text, easy to read and search
- **Mind Map** - Visual concept mapping
- **Study Materials** - The main learning interface (see below)

**Study Materials has 6 tabs:**
- **Overview** - Main topic, key objectives, core concepts
- **Questions** - Practice quiz with different difficulty levels
- **Explanations** - Deep dives on important concepts
- **Summary** - Essential points + exam focus (what to memorize, likely questions)
- **Study Time** - Pomodoro timer integrated with this lecture
- **Materials** - Access files and exports

*Think: Everything needed to master a lecture in one place*

### 4. **Schedule**
Calendar view of classes, deadlines, study sessions

### 5. **Pomodoro**
Focus timer to track study sessions

### 6. **Exam Prep**
Simulate exams, practice questions

### 7. **Stats**
Track study hours, completed lectures, scores, streaks

## How Students Use Mindsy

### The Typical Journey:

1. **Attend Lecture** (or find lecture video online)
2. **Upload to Mindsy** (while doing other things - processing happens in background)
3. **Open Student Desk** when ready to study
4. **Review** using transcript, mind map, or study materials
5. **Practice** with quiz questions
6. **Focus** using Pomodoro timer
7. **Prepare for Exam** using summary tab and exam prep
8. **Pass Exam** 🎉
